Still air

We design for still air and steady flow.

Gusts and eddies are kept out.

Calm air is predictable, and so is everything in it.

A leaf on a steady breeze drifts in a straight line.

Nothing tumbles. Nothing wanders. No wonder.

Into the wind

Let the wind gust, and eddies spin up.

Release a leaf into it, and it loops and veers.

Trace its flight, and the wind draws a shape.
